Paul and Silas have spent the night
praying and singing in prison.

Then, in a single moment,
the atmosphere shifts.

An earthquake shakes the foundations.

The prison doors open.

Chains are loosed.

The jailer awakens
in fear and confusion,

certain that all hope has been lost.

But instead of fleeing,
Paul calls out with reassurance.

And in the middle
of that shaken and desperate moment,
the jailer asks a question
that reaches far beyond the prison walls.

A question that echoes
through every human heart.

## Scripture Reading

<sup class="verse-num">30</sup> And brought them out, and said, Sirs, what must I do to be saved?

## Devotional Reflection

Something has shifted within the jailer.

Moments earlier,
he was simply carrying out his duties.

Now,
everything feels different.

The earthquake shook
more than the prison.

It shook his heart awake.

And when that awakening comes,
the deepest questions begin to rise.

Not questions about success.

Or comfort.

Or status.

But questions about the soul.

Many people move through life
distracted by smaller concerns.

Schedules.

Responsibilities.

Daily pressures.

But beneath all of it,
there’s often a deeper longing.

A desire for peace.

For forgiveness.

For meaning.

For reconciliation with God.

Sometimes it takes a disruption
to bring those questions to the surface.

And when they rise,
they should *not* be ignored.

Today,
be honest before God.

Don’t hide your fears,
your doubts,
or your spiritual hunger.

Bring your real questions
into the light.

God isn’t threatened by sincerity.

And often,
the beginning of transformation
is simply the willingness to ask.

A searching heart
is already moving toward Him.

## Closing Prayer

*Lord,*

*There are questions within my heart*
*that go deeper than daily concerns.*

*Questions about purpose,*
*peace,*
*forgiveness,*
*and the condition of my soul.*

*Help me not to silence those deeper longings*
*through distraction or fear.*

*Give me the courage to seek You honestly.*

*When life shakes my assumptions,*
*draw me closer rather than farther away.*

*Awaken within me a deeper hunger for truth,*
*for wisdom,*
*and for Your presence.*

*And remind me that You welcome*
*every sincere heart that turns toward You.*

*Teach me to bring my deepest questions*
*into Your light,*
*trusting that Your grace is greater*
*than my uncertainty.*

*Because the soul finds rest*
*when it finally turns toward You.*

*In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit,*

*Amen.*
