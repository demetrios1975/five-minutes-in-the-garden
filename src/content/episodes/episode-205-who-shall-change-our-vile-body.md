One of the undeniable realities of life is that our bodies change.

The energy we once had begins to fade.

Scars remain.

Illness comes without invitation.

Strength that once seemed effortless gradually gives way to weakness.

If we live long enough, every one of us becomes acquainted with the frailty of our earthly bodies.

For some, those reminders come early in life.

For others, they arrive much later.

But all of us eventually discover that this present life is not the final chapter of our story.

Paul understood that reality.

After reminding us that our true citizenship is in heaven, he ends this section of Philippians with a breathtaking promise.

Christ is not only preparing a place for us.

***He is preparing us for that place.***

## Scripture Reading

> <sup class="verse-num">21</sup> Who shall change our vile body, that it may be fashioned like unto His glorious body, according to the working whereby He is able even to subdue all things unto Himself.

## Devotional Reading

Paul speaks honestly about our present condition.

Our bodies are subject to weakness.

To sickness.

To pain.

And ultimately, to death.

Yet he refuses to let that be the final word.

The same Lord who rose from the dead has promised to transform those who belong to Him.

The resurrection is not merely the continuation of life as we know it.

It is the renewal of all things.

The broken will be made whole.

The weary will find rest.

The mortal will put on immortality.

Creation itself will share in that final renewal.

This is ***not*** wishful thinking.

It is the promise of the risen Christ,

whose own resurrection is the firstfruits of what is yet to come.

Today, thank God for the body He has given you.

Care for it wisely.

Use it to serve others.

Offer your hands to help.

Your voice to encourage.

Your strength—whatever measure you have—to glorify God.

But do not place your ultimate hope in physical health or earthly strength.

Place your hope in Christ.

The day is coming when every weakness will give way to glory.

Every tear will be wiped away.

Every scar redeemed.

Until that day,

live with joyful expectation.

The best is not behind you.

For those who belong to Christ,

the best is still to come.

## Closing Prayer

*Lord Jesus,*

*Thank You for the hope that reaches beyond the limits of this present life.*

*When I become discouraged by weakness,*

*remind me of Your promise.*

*When my body grows tired,*

*strengthen my spirit.*

*When I face pain,*

*fill me with the quiet confidence that You are making all things new.*

*Help me to use the life You have given me faithfully,*

*serving You with gratitude in every season.*

*And keep my heart fixed upon the glorious day when You will complete the work You have begun,*

*raising Your people to share forever in the glory of Your Kingdom.*

*Until that day,*

*let my hope remain steadfast,*

*my faith unwavering,*

*and my eyes fixed upon You.*

*For You are the risen Lord,*

*the conqueror of death,*

*and the One who will transform all things according to Your glorious power.*

*In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit,*

*Amen.*
