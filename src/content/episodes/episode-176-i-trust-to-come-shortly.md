There’s a difference
between confidence
and presumption.

Presumption says,

“I know exactly
what will happen.”

Confidence says,

“I know the One
who holds tomorrow.”

On the surface,

Paul had every reason
to be uncertain.

He was writing
from prison.

He didn’t know
when he would be released.

He didn’t know
what the coming days
would bring.

Yet he continued
to make plans.

Not with pride.

Not with certainty
in himself.

But with confidence
in the Lord.

His hope rested,

not in changing circumstances,

but in the *unchanging faithfulness of Christ.*

## Scripture Reading

<sup class="verse-num">24</sup> But I trust in the Lord that I also myself shall come shortly.

## Devotional Reflection

Notice where Paul
places his confidence.

“I trust in the Lord.”

He doesn’t trust
in favorable circumstances.

He doesn’t trust
in the decisions
of earthly rulers.

He doesn’t even trust
in his own wisdom.

His confidence rests
*entirely*
in Christ.

This is the kind of confidence
God invites us to have.

Not a confidence
that assumes life
will always unfold
according to our plans.

But a confidence
that knows God is faithful,

whatever tomorrow
may bring.

Faith does not remove
uncertainty.

It gives us
Someone trustworthy
*in the midst of it.*

Most of us enjoy knowing
what comes next.

We make calendars.

Schedules.

Lists.

We plan vacations,

family gatherings,

and important milestones.

There’s wisdom
in preparing
for the future.

But there’s also wisdom
in remembering
that every tomorrow
belongs to God.

When we hold our plans
too tightly,

disappointment
can easily become
discouragement.

When we place them
in God’s hands,

we discover
a deeper peace.

The future
no longer depends
upon our ability
to control every outcome.

It rests
in the hands
of a loving Father

who sees the end
from the beginning.

Today,

think about something
that lies ahead.

A meeting.

A decision.

A conversation.

A season of life
you cannot yet
fully see.

Pray over it.

Prepare wisely.

Then release it
into God’s care.

Walk faithfully
through the responsibilities
of today,

trusting that
when tomorrow arrives,

the Lord
will already be there,

providing the grace
you need
for that moment.

One faithful step
is enough.

God will guide
the next one
when the time comes.

## Closing Prayer

*Lord,*

*Tomorrow belongs to You.*

*Long before I awaken,*

*You are already there.*

*Long before I understand*
*what lies ahead,*

*Your wisdom*
*has prepared the way.*

*Thank You*
*that I do not have to carry*
*the weight of the future*
*upon my shoulders.*

*Teach me*
*to plan with humility,*

*to hope with confidence,*

*and to trust*
*without fear.*

*Keep me faithful*
*in today’s responsibilities,*

*knowing that today’s obedience*
*is the best preparation*
*for tomorrow.*

*And whatever the future holds,*

*may I never lose sight*
*of the One who holds me.*

*For my confidence*
*is not found*
*in knowing what comes next,*

*but in knowing You.*

*In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit,*

*Amen.*
