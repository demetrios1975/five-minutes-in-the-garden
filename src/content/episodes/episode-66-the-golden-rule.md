## Scripture Reading

<sup class="verse-num">12</sup> Therefore all things whatsoever ye would that men should do to you, do ye even so to them: for this is the law and the prophets.

---

## Devotional Reflection

Throughout the Sermon on the Mount, Jesus teaches us how to live with humility, trust, and devotion.

But He also summarizes the heart of God’s law in one simple sentence.

It is often called **the Golden Rule.**

Yet its simplicity should not hide its power.

The Golden Rule moves beyond intention.

It calls for **action.**

Treat others not according to how they treat you — but according to how you wish to be treated.

This transforms relationships.

• Mercy replaces resentment.  
• Kindness replaces indifference.

When we place ourselves in another person’s position, compassion grows.

We begin to see their struggles more clearly.

We become slower to judge and quicker to help.

This is the heart of Christian love.

Today, practice the Golden Rule deliberately.

Offer patience where you expect impatience.  
Offer encouragement where you expect criticism.

Small acts of kindness reflect the kingdom of God.
