Yesterday,
we saw the believers in Antioch
ministering to the Lord
through prayer,
worship,
and fasting.

And in that atmosphere of devotion,
the Holy Spirit spoke.

Barnabas and Saul
were set apart
for a new work.

Now comes the next step.

Not merely hearing the call,

but going.

And Scripture makes something important clear
from the very beginning.

They’re not being sent out alone.

They are being sent forth
by the Holy Ghost.

## Scripture Reading

<sup class="verse-num">4</sup> So they, being sent forth by the Holy Ghost, departed unto Seleucia; and from thence they sailed to Cyprus.

## Devotional Reflection

There’s a difference
between being driven by pressure
and being led by God.

Barnabas and Saul
aren’t rushing ahead
out of ambition or impulse.

They’re responding
to the Spirit’s direction.

This matters deeply.

Because the Christian life
is not meant to be lived
through self-reliance alone.

God does not merely point toward a path
and leave us there.

He guides.

He strengthens.

He accompanies.

We often want certainty
before we begin.

A complete plan.

A guaranteed outcome.

Every detail in place.

But many times,
God gives direction
one step at a time.

Barnabas and Saul
begin the journey
without knowing everything ahead.

Yet they move forward
because they trust
the One sending them.

And that trust
becomes enough.

Today,
pay attention
to where God may be leading you.

Perhaps it’s a conversation.

A responsibility.

A season of change.

A quiet act of obedience.

Don’t focus only
on the uncertainty ahead.

Remember who goes with you.

You are not asked
to carry the journey alone.

The same Spirit who calls
also sustains.

## Closing Prayer

*Lord,*

*There are moments*
*when the path ahead*
*feels uncertain to me.*

*I want clarity,*
*security,*
*and complete understanding*
*before I move forward.*

*Yet You often lead*
*one step at a time.*

*Teach me to trust Your guidance.*

*When You call me forward,*
*give me willingness to follow.*

*When fear or hesitation rises,*
*remind me that I do not walk alone.*

*Let Your Spirit guide my decisions,*
*steady my heart,*
*and strengthen me*
*for whatever lies ahead.*

*Keep me from being driven only by pressure,*
*anxiety,*
*or ambition.*

*Instead, teach me to move with peace,*
*attentiveness,*
*and trust in Your direction.*

*Because wherever You send me,*
*Your presence goes before me.*

*In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit,*

*Amen.*
