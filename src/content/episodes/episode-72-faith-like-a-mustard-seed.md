## Scripture Reading

<sup class="verse-num">20</sup> If ye have faith as a grain of mustard seed… nothing shall be impossible unto you.

---

## Devotional Reflection

Sometimes we believe that strong faith must *feel* strong.

But Jesus teaches something very different.

Faith doesn’t need to be large.

It needs to be real.

A mustard seed is tiny.

Almost insignificant.

But it contains life.

Our faith does not need to be impressive.

It needs to be alive.

Lent reveals our weakness.

But that does not disqualify us.

Even fragile faith, when offered honestly, becomes powerful in God’s hands.

Today, bring your faith as it is.

• Not perfected.  
• Not polished.  
• Just real.

God works with what is living.

## Closing Prayer

*Lord,  
Strengthen the faith within me, even when it feels small.  
Receive what I offer You, however imperfect it may be, and bring it to life through Your grace.  
Help me trust You not because I feel strong, but because You are faithful.  
Nurture the seed of faith within my heart, that it may grow steadily and take root in every part of my life.  
In moments of doubt, remind me that You are present.  
In moments of weakness, remind me that You are near.  
And let my faith grow into something that reflects Your power and Your love.  
In the name of the Father, and of the Son, and of the Holy Spirit,  
Amen.*
