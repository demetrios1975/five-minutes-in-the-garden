Light is meant to reveal.

To guide.

To help others
find their way.

Yesterday,

Paul reminded us
that believers
are called to shine
as lights in the world.

Today he tells us
how that light
continues to shine.

Not through our own ideas.

Not through our own accomplishments.

But by holding forth
the Word of Life.

In a world filled
with countless opinions,

changing values,

and uncertain voices,

God has given us
something that never changes.

His Word.

And it continues
to bring life
to every heart
that receives it.

## Scripture Reading

<sup class="verse-num">16</sup> Holding forth the word of life; that I may rejoice in the day of Christ, that I have not run in vain, neither laboured in vain.

## Devotional Reflection

Paul describes the Gospel
as the Word of Life.

It's more than information.

More than wise teaching.

It's God's *living message.*

It reveals Christ.

It strengthens faith.

It offers hope.

And it leads us
into eternal life.

We cannot give life
to others
through our own strength.

But we can faithfully
hold forth the Word
that has first given life
to us.

As we remain rooted
in Scripture,

our lives become steady.

Our hope
becomes stronger.

And our witness
becomes clearer.

Imagine someone
walking through the darkness
carrying a lantern.

The lantern
does not create the path.

It simply makes
the path visible.

That is our calling
as followers of Christ.

We are not asked
to invent the truth.

We are not asked
to make the Gospel
more appealing.

We are simply called
to carry it faithfully.

There are people
all around us
searching for hope.

Searching for peace.

Searching for purpose.

The Word of God
continues to illuminate
the path
that leads to Christ.

When we hold fast to it,

and hold it forth
with love,

we become faithful bearers
of that light.

Today,

spend a few quiet moments
in Scripture.

Read slowly.

Listen carefully.

Ask the Lord
to speak to your heart.

Then carry that Word
with you
throughout the day.

Perhaps a single verse.

A promise.

A reminder
of His faithfulness.

Allow God's Word
to shape your thoughts,

guide your decisions,

and encourage your heart.

The more deeply
His Word
lives within us,

the more naturally
it shines through us.

## Closing Prayer

*Heavenly Father,*

*Thank You for giving us*
*Your living Word.*

*Thank You that through it*
*we have come to know Christ,*

*to receive hope,*

*and to find the path*
*that leads to life.*

*Plant Your Word deeply*
*within my heart.*

*May it shape my thoughts,*

*guide my choices,*

*and strengthen my faith.*

*When I am uncertain,*

*let Your truth steady me.*

*When I grow weary,*

*let Your promises encourage me.*

*And when others*
*are searching for hope,*

*help me gently point them,*

*not to myself,*

*but to Your Son,*

*the true Word of Life.*

*May I faithfully carry*
*the light*
*You have entrusted to me,*

*until the day*
*I stand joyfully*
*in Your presence.*

*In the name of the Father,*

*and of the Son,*

*and of the Holy Spirit,*

*Amen.*
